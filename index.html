How does a Data Savings Plan work?

OWN YOUR DATA

Sign up & request your data

With dWallet, you can request your data from companies you interact with. From streaming to shopping, the data you generate will finally belong to you.

SAVE YOUR DATA

Save & manage it

Contribute your data to a Data Savings Plan and grow its value, much like money builds in a traditional bank account. You decide what data to add and what you’re open to licensing.

RECEIVE OFFERS

Earn from its value

You’re always in control. When companies want to license your data, you decide which offers to accept. Say yes, and you’ll get paid directly in your dWallet. Save it or cash out anytime.

PAY WITH DATA (COMING SOON)

Transact using its value

Soon you’ll be able to make purchases using the balance from your Data Savings Plan. Using a QR code, dPay enables you to pay with the value of your data.

DrumWave doesn’t share, sell or store your data.
